## Scope

Rank timely story angles by significance, novelty, relevance and channel; record selection reasons and compare against recently used angles.

## Done when

The bot selects a supported story without manual topic selection and avoids repetitive or duplicate posts.

---

## From the Development Guide

### Avoiding repetitive content

*Source: DEVELOPMENT_GUIDE.md 2.2 Post Types, Configurations & Triggers*

Weekly posts should not repeat the same information without a meaningful new development.

For example, the first post might introduce the players; subsequent posts might cover rating movements, qualification stories, preparation, the match format or significant news.

Maintain a history of published topics and compare new candidates against it.

If there is insufficient fresh information, the system may select a genuinely different evergreen angle or flag that no worthwhile post is available. It should not invent developments merely to satisfy a schedule.

### Recommended AI tasks

*Source: DEVELOPMENT_GUIDE.md 2.3 Data Sources & Content Generation*

- Interpreting collected research.
- Identifying potential stories.
- Ranking story angles.
- Creating headlines.
- Writing captions.
- Producing three candidate versions.
- Suggesting appropriate visual templates.
- Reviewing drafts against the research package.
- Analysing human feedback.

### Content quality requirements

*Source: DEVELOPMENT_GUIDE.md 2.3 Data Sources & Content Generation*

The generated content must:

- Be factually accurate and current.
- Avoid misleading headlines.
- Use the correct player names, titles and countries.
- Distinguish classical, rapid and blitz ratings.
- Clearly distinguish predictions from confirmed facts.
- Avoid repeating recently published content.
- Respect each channel's formatting and length requirements.
- Use natural, engaging language.
- Avoid unnecessary promotional language.
- Avoid plagiarism or reproducing substantial copyrighted text.

The content generator should return structured data rather than only a block of prose.

Example:

```json
{
  "postType": "1.1",
  "angle": "Championship rating comparison",
  "candidates": [
    {
      "version": "A",
      "headline": "Championship Preview",
      "caption": "Example caption...",
      "claims": ["claim-001", "claim-002"],
      "visualTemplate": "player-versus"
    }
  ]
}
```

This is an illustrative partial response; the production schema must also represent all three versions, source references, media requirements and validation results.

## From the Feature Definition

### Story Selection & Fact Verification

*Source: FEATURES.md 1.2.3 Story Selection & Fact Verification*

Identifies worthwhile stories, selects an appropriate angle and validates factual claims before content generation. SMB must determine what is newsworthy without a human supplying each topic.

### Content selection and generation

*Source: FEATURES.md 1.2.3 Story Selection & Fact Verification*

SMB must decide what makes a worthwhile social media post.

Simply retrieving a chess article and rewriting it is not sufficient.

The system should evaluate potential stories based on relevance, timeliness, significance, novelty, audience interest and suitability for the configured channel.

### Story selection

*Source: FEATURES.md 1.2.3 Story Selection & Fact Verification*

For a World Championship post, potential angles might include:

- The champion's rating movement since winning the title.
- The challenger's recent performance.
- A major qualification milestone.
- A significant difference between the players.
- An interesting historical comparison.
- A newly announced match detail.

SMB should rank these angles and choose the strongest one supported by the available evidence.

Store the reason for selecting each angle so reviewers can understand the decision.
