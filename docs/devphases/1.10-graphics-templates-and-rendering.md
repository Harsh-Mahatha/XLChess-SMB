## From the Development Guide

### World Championship trial — reference

*Source: DEVELOPMENT_GUIDE.md 2.4 Image Sourcing & Graphics*

Reference link: **Endgame AI (@EndgameaiChess) on X**

Illustrative layout reference only; the original Gukesh–Sindarov trial artwork and supplied photographs remain the intended design reference.

The trial graphic included two real player photographs, a neutral grey background, player names, national flags, classical ratings, a prominent VS, a strong headline and championship information.

The important design concept was a compelling factual story:

> Can the challenger defeat the World Champion after a significant decline in the champion's rating?

The ratings and rating decline were calculated from published FIDE classical rating data rather than invented.

For production, all time-sensitive numbers must be refreshed and verified at the time of generation.

### Graphics technology

*Source: DEVELOPMENT_GUIDE.md 2.4 Image Sourcing & Graphics*

Recommended initial approach:

- HTML/CSS or SVG templates for layouts.
- Sharp for image resizing, cropping and composition.
- A server-side renderer such as Playwright for producing final images from HTML templates.
- AI for choosing a visual concept, suggesting layout and generating captions.
- Deterministic rendering for the final photographs, flags, text and statistics.

This is preferable to generating the entire poster as one AI image because it gives us exact control over the photographs, factual text and output dimensions.

AI-generated backgrounds or decorative assets may be used where useful, provided they do not modify original photographs or introduce misleading visual information.

## From the Feature Definition

### Template-based graphics

*Source: FEATURES.md 1.2.4 Content & Graphics Production*

Build reusable templates for common social media formats.

Initial templates should include:

1. Player vs Player
2. Upcoming Tournament
3. Tournament Result
4. Player Ranking
5. Rating Movement
6. Chess Puzzle
7. Chess News
8. XLChess Product Update

Templates should define layout regions, typography, colours, image placement and supported aspect ratios.

A template must be able to adapt to different text lengths and player photograph dimensions without breaking the design.

### Preserve original photographs

*Source: FEATURES.md 1.2.4 Content & Graphics Production*

This is an explicit requirement based on the World Championship trial.

The original photographs must remain visually unchanged.

Permitted operations:

- Crop
- Resize
- Rotate
- Position
- Place inside a frame
- Arrange with other design elements

Not permitted:

- AI reconstruction of faces
- Retouching
- Changing facial expressions
- Changing clothing or hair
- Recolouring the photographs
- Replacing or generating portions of a photograph
- Applying generative image transformations to the player

The preferred implementation is to use the original photograph directly in a compositing system.

For example, use Sharp or another deterministic image-processing library to crop and position the original image within a template.

Do not send the entire finished graphic through a generative image model when this could redraw the people.
