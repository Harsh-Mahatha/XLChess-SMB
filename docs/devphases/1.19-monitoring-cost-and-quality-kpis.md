## From the Development Guide

### Cost and efficiency

*Source: DEVELOPMENT_GUIDE.md 2.6 Testing, Operations & Delivery*

SMB is intended to reduce operating costs, so the implementation must be economical.

Throughput is determined by available worker capacity, processing time and job priority; there is no fixed daily post quota.

Avoid expensive infrastructure and unnecessary AI calls.

Recommended optimisations include:

- Cache chess data instead of retrieving it repeatedly.
- Reuse research packages across related posts.
- Use a lower-cost model for simple classification and extraction where quality permits.
- Reserve more capable models for complex research and editorial generation.
- Generate images through templates rather than regenerating entire graphics with AI.
- Reuse verified image assets where their licences permit.
- Process posts asynchronously.
- Track the cost of each generated post.

Measure both infrastructure/API cost and human review time.

A post that costs very little to generate but requires 30 minutes of manual correction is not meeting the objective.

### Monitoring and performance

*Source: DEVELOPMENT_GUIDE.md 2.6 Testing, Operations & Delivery*

SMB needs operational metrics and quality metrics.

### Operational metrics

*Source: DEVELOPMENT_GUIDE.md 2.6 Testing, Operations & Delivery*

Track scheduled opportunities, successful generations, failed jobs, review queue size, publishing success, duplicate prevention and average processing time.

### Content quality metrics

*Source: DEVELOPMENT_GUIDE.md 2.6 Testing, Operations & Delivery*

| Primary quality KPI | Long-term target |
|---|---|
| **Unedited approval rate** — posts approved without any changes | **10 days** — consecutive days without corrections |

Other important metrics:

- Average human review time.
- Average number of regeneration requests.
- Factual correction rate.
- Image replacement rate.
- Percentage of posts rejected.
- Cost per approved post.
- Percentage of posts published successfully.
- Performance by post type and channel.

The ten-day target should be evaluated only across days with the required posting volume, without excluding difficult or rejected posts from the calculation.

A useful dashboard should show which post types consistently produce acceptable content and which require improvement.

### Management through XLChess

*Source: DEVELOPMENT_GUIDE.md 2.1 Development Approach & Architecture*

After initial pairing, routine administration must be available through the XLChess web application: worker online/offline state, current job, queue and priorities, completed and failed jobs, processing time, health and GPU usage, logs, pause/resume, retries, concurrency and resource limits, model selection/downloads, prompts, configuration versions and controlled worker updates. Configuration changes must reach the PC without requiring the owner to log in and edit files.

## From the Feature Definition

### Quality objective

*Source: FEATURES.md 1.1 Overview*

The system should improve until every original SMB-generated post can be approved without modification for ten consecutive days at the required posting volume. Human approval remains mandatory; the quality target does not authorise automatic publishing.

### Learning from corrections

*Source: FEATURES.md 1.2.6 Publishing, Monitoring & Feedback*

Every edit should be recorded.

Examples include changes to the headline, image selection, caption wording, layout, factual claims or visual hierarchy.

The system should distinguish subjective creative improvements from factual corrections.

Feedback must be recorded and used to improve templates, prompts and post-type configurations; model fine-tuning may be evaluated when useful.

Measure how frequently generated content is accepted without changes, and which corrections recur.

The system monitors production and publishing success, content quality, correction frequency and the unedited approval rate.
