## Scope

Search approved photo sources, record original URLs, authorship, licence versions, commercial/modification permissions and attribution obligations; reject uncertain rights.

## Done when

Each chosen real photo has a verified licence and recorded attribution; unusable images cannot enter publication.

### Image licence verification

*Source: DEVELOPMENT_GUIDE.md 2.4 Image Sourcing & Graphics*

Every photograph must be verified individually.

Store:

- Original image URL and source page.
- Photographer and copyright holder, where identified.
- Licence type and version.
- Whether commercial use is permitted.
- Whether modifications are permitted.
- Attribution requirements.
- Licence URL and verification date.
- Any share-alike or other obligations.
- A copy or record of the licence evidence.

A photograph being visible on a free website does not automatically mean XLChess can use it.

Do not use images with non-commercial restrictions for XLChess promotional content without separate permission. Other restrictions, including no-derivatives requirements, must be assessed against the intended use.

If suitable licensing cannot be established, reject the photograph.

## From the Feature Definition

### Image sourcing and graphics generation

*Source: FEATURES.md 1.2.4 Content & Graphics Production*

This is one of the most important parts of SMB.

The developer must understand that the goal is not simply to ask an image-generation model to create a picture.

We need a system capable of finding real, legally usable photographs and assembling them into professional social media graphics.

Our World Championship trial featuring Gukesh and Sindarov demonstrated the desired presentation, but also highlighted the importance of preserving original photographs.

### Separate image selection from graphic composition

*Source: FEATURES.md 1.2.4 Content & Graphics Production*

The system needs two distinct components.

**Image selection** finds suitable photographs, verifies licences, evaluates resolution and composition, and chooses the best candidates.

**Graphic composition** combines the selected photographs with backgrounds, typography, flags, ratings, statistics and other visual elements.

This separation allows SMB to use real photographs without altering them.
